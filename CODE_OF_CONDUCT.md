# Code of conduct

Pathloom welcomes anyone who wants to use it, report a problem or improve it, whatever your experience, background or language.

## What we expect

- Be kind and patient, with newcomers above all.
- Criticise code and ideas, never people.
- Assume good intent, and ask before you conclude.
- Keep discussions on topic and in English or Ukrainian.

## What we do not accept

Harassment, insults, discriminatory jokes, personal attacks, sharing someone's private information, and sexual content.

## If something goes wrong

Contact the maintainer, [@XxArDy](https://github.com/XxArDy). The maintainer reads every report, keeps it confidential, and
may warn, remove content, or block someone from the project.

This applies to the repository, issues, discussions, pull requests and any other project space.
